ABOUT

A small site with a big curiosity.

LinguaBloom is an independent educational project about language, linguistics, culture and learning.

What we publish

We create original explainers, examples and study-friendly guides. The goal is not to make every topic sound complicated. It is to make useful knowledge easier to understand while keeping the interesting details intact.

Our approach

We value clarity, context and responsible sourcing. Articles should add something of their own rather than simply repackage another website's work.

Who it is for

Students, language learners, teachers, readers interested in African languages and anyone who has ever wondered why humans say things the way they do.
